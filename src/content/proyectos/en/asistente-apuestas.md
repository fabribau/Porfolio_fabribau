An intelligent conversational system developed as my Informatics Engineering Degree Thesis at Universidad Nacional de San Luis (UNSL), published at **CoNaIISI 2025** and the **Mendoza Mental Health Congress**.

- **Rigorous Evaluation:** Evaluation metrics for faithfulness and relevance.
