## Project Overview

An intelligent conversational system developed as our **Informatics Engineering Degree Thesis (Capstone Project)** at Universidad Nacional de San Luis (UNSL), graded with the highest academic mark **10/10 (Distinction)** by the examination board. The project addresses the alarming rise of digital gambling addiction and unauthorized betting platforms among adolescents and university students.

The system was custom-engineered for the **University Problematic Consumption and Addictive Behavior Prevention Program** of UNSL, with an intellectual property registration in progress.

---

### Background & Societal Problem

The widespread growth of virtual casinos, digital wallets, and sports betting platforms has caused a critical increase in behavioral addictions. Due to the lack of accessible and non-stigmatizing tools, this assistant provides an interactive, private, and empathetic digital channel to:

- Detect early behavioral warning signs and risk patterns.
- Deliver scientifically validated educational content on cognitive biases in gambling (illusion of control, gambler's fallacy).
- Guide users toward psychological support networks and professional healthcare channels.

---

### Technical Architecture & RAG Pipeline

1. **Ingestion & Semantic Chunking Pipeline:** Automated processing and indexing of clinical guidelines, prevention manuals, and mental health protocols.
2. **Embeddings & Vector Database:** High-dimensional vector indexing enabling strict contextual retrieval tailored to user inputs.
3. **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG):** The LLM grounds all responses on verified clinical and preventive literature, effectively eliminating hallucinations and ensuring factual accuracy.
4. **Safety Guardrails & Ethical Boundaries:** Real-time safeguards ensuring no clinical diagnoses are made, prioritizing empathy, de-escalation, and referral to professional counselors.
5. **Technology Stack:** Modular backend built with **Python / FastAPI**, integrated via **Vercel AI SDK**, reactive frontend in **Next.js**, and scalable vector storage.
