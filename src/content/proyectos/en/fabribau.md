## About the project

This website itself is a meta and self-referential project, crafted to reflect my identity as a software engineer and educator.

### Key features:
- **Neobrutalism:** Vibrant palette, bold borders, and solid hard shadows.
- **Bilingual:** Full Spanish and English routing without FOUC.
- **Extreme Performance:** Powered by Astro island architecture and Cloudflare Pages SSG.
